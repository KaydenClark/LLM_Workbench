# Continuity and evidence boundaries: the vocabulary explained

These words mark where continuity and evidence stop: what a transport, a promotion, a host check or a compatibility range does and does not establish.

The [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md#continuity-and-evidence-boundaries) owns each definition; this article explains how each word differs from its neighbours. It authorizes nothing.

## Workbench connection identity

Clones/worktrees share it; independent rooms differ. It is distinct from each visible, type-scoped artifact identifier.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Continuity and evidence boundaries](../../GLOSSARY.md#continuity-and-evidence-boundaries).

## Private session transport

Private Git retention changes recoverability, not the record's authority or durable project ownership.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Continuity and evidence boundaries](../../GLOSSARY.md#continuity-and-evidence-boundaries).

## Direct promotion

It is not merely a copy or commit.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Continuity and evidence boundaries](../../GLOSSARY.md#continuity-and-evidence-boundaries).

## Configured-host capability

It does not establish machine enforcement or model reliability.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Continuity and evidence boundaries](../../GLOSSARY.md#continuity-and-evidence-boundaries).

## Core compatibility

A version difference alone is not proof of incompatibility.
