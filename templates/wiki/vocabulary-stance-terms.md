# Stance terms: the vocabulary explained

A stance is the job an agent does inside its assigned role. Each section says what one stance does and what it does not do.

The [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms) owns each definition; this article explains how each word differs from its neighbours. It authorizes nothing.

## Stance

Role answers where responsibility applies; stance answers what job is performed there. A stance neither grants authority nor creates independence or an agent by being loaded.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

## Spec Planner

A Dispatcher may dispatch Workers to help author Tasks, then reconciles their drafts and hands the plan to Spec Manager. Its operating capability is specified separately from the Dispatcher role.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

## Spec Manager

A Dispatcher manages safe parallel work, hand-backs and assembled verification, escalating cross-Spec coordination to the Director. Its operating capability is specified separately from Spec Planner.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

## Builder

Implementation includes relevant review and verification.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

## Auditor

An audit does not authorize repairs or release.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

## Reviewer

At a Spec's or landmark's Verify it runs in a separate context; it does not quietly repair the candidate.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

## Reconciler

It neither manufactures completion nor duplicates truth in a universal handoff.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Stance terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).
