# Workbench meanings of AI coding terms: the vocabulary explained

Two general AI coding words carry a distinct meaning in the Workbench. The rest of the AI coding vocabulary stays general reference in the [AI coding reference](ai-coding-reference.md).

The [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md#workbench-meanings-of-ai-coding-terms) owns each definition; this article explains how each word differs from its neighbours. It authorizes nothing.

## Automated review

Separate-context review is an earlier name for it. In this Workbench it judges a completed destination against its Map: it runs on a Spec, sometimes a landmark, or the Workbench as a whole against its decision records and Blueprint, and never a Task: the sum of the completed Tasks shows at the destination. It is how the Review verb is carried out. It is neither an Automated check (which has no judgement) nor Human review.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Workbench meanings of AI coding terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#workbench-meanings-of-ai-coding-terms).

## Grilling

The Workbench differs from the dictionary here: a handoff can be written in the middle of a grilling session so that another agent can dig into one question while the grilling agent's context is preserved. Align is usually done by grilling; the `grilling` skill owns the method.

Definition: [GLOSSARY.md, Workbench meanings of AI coding terms](../../GLOSSARY.md#workbench-meanings-of-ai-coding-terms).
