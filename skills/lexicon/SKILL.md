# Lexicon

The Lexicon is the **map**: each term names a concept and the doc that owns it.

Read the nearest `LEXICON.md` whole. Its terms are defined against each other,
so one read alone loses the distinction it was written to hold.

Then work from it:

- **Speak it.** Use its terms exactly, in your own sentences, from the first
  reply on. Prefer the current name, and say so once when a retired one
  surfaces — in an older doc, in your draft, or in what the owner just said.
- **Travel it.** Follow a term's link to the doc behind it rather than
  searching for that doc.
- **Keep open what is open.** It records unsettled questions beside settled
  ones. Reporting one as settled is the failure this exists to prevent.

Supplying vocabulary is the whole job: a flow skill running alongside keeps its
own sequence and its own writes. When the map itself is wrong, name the gap and
route it — `/ubiquitous-language` to promote vocabulary, `/domain-modeling` to
settle a term as a decision forms.
