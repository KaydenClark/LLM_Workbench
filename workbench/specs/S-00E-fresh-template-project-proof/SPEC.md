## Outcome

A fresh copy of the Workbench Template can become an independent useful project
through the delivered evidence, grilling, and Genesis path.

## Desired Behavior

The proof starts from a clean copy, preserves new-room identity, exercises a
small real project change and its own tools, and demonstrates a safe saved-note
continuation without relying on the developer checkout or personal catalog.

## Decisions And Contracts

- This final proof is distinct from the v3.2 Template-update acceptance.
- A copy is an independent room with a new identity; a clone of the same room
  retains its connection identity only where the established identity contract
  says so.

## Non-Goals

- Treating a tour, hand-copied tree, or fixture-only test as end-to-end proof.
- Deploying to an unnamed real project.
