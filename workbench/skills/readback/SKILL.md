# Readback

Restate the meaning the owner gave you, not a design you wish they had chosen.
Keep it short enough to correct in one reply.

1. State the understood intent, constraints and intended result. Keep the
   owner's reasons separate from your inference. At a grilling exit, keep
   its Question / Answer / Why / Impact form.
2. Name assumptions, missing decisions and consequential conflicts as pending.
   Add no unstated defaults, costs, outputs or design commitments. Omit an
   empty list rather than invent a question.
3. If the request includes a next action, name that action and its endpoint.
   Otherwise the endpoint is this readback. Ask: "Does this match what you
   mean, or what should I correct?"

A correction gets a revised readback of the affected meaning. Silence is not
confirmation. New meaning stays pending until the owner's explicit confirmation;
do not promote or implement work that depends on it beforehand.

Confirmation carries the understood direction only to the endpoint the owner
requested; it adds no authority beyond that scope and needs no second invented
approval. A handoff does not reopen settled decisions or suspend work already
authorized. Clarify only the changed or missing decision and continue independent
authorized work. A request only for readback ends with the confirmed readback.
