# Project: the work a Workbench exists to deliver

A Project is the product or body of work a Workbench exists to deliver. The
canonical definition is the [glossary
entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#workbench-room-and-artifacts).

**What it means here.** Every Project has exactly one Workbench of its own. The Project is the work; the Workbench is the management system around it. Room names the same project seen as the place its work happens.

**Neighbouring words.** The [Workbench](dictionary-workbench.md) is the
management system around it, one-to-one from the project's side; the
[Room](dictionary-room.md) is the same project seen as a place. The
[Owner](dictionary-owner.md) is the person whose ideas it realizes, and its
[Blueprint](dictionary-blueprint.md) summarizes the direction it is heading.

**In use.** LLM Workbench is itself a Project, with its own Workbench in this
repository: its work is the Workbench Template and the tools, skills and
controls that ship with it ([the producer and product
decision](../docs/ddr/000P-llm-workbench-is-the-producer-and-the-workbench-template-is-its-product.md)).
A room made from the Template is a different Project with a Workbench of its
own.
