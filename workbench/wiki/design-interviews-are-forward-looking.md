# Design interviews are forward-looking

In a design grilling, when the owner describes how something should work, take
it as the design direction and build on it. Do not answer "nothing is labeled
that today" or "unverified" as if correcting him: the absence is why the design
conversation is happening. On 2026-09-22, pointing out that no Spec was yet
labeled backlog, after he described set-aside work as the backlog, read to him
as the agent not understanding the goal of the conversation.

Current-state facts belong in the [notepad](skill-notepad.md) only when they
change a migration cost or reveal a real conflict with a locked decision.
Otherwise follow the framing and ask the next design question. See
[Grilling](skill-grilling.md) for the interview itself.
