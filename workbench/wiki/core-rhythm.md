# Core rhythm

The owner's governing principle for the Workbench: it makes complex,
human-directed work executable within limited AI context windows by balancing
two needs.

- **Context conservation:** keep the active window focused on what the current
  step needs.
- **Context continuity:** preserve understanding outside the window, so clearing
  or resuming never requires reconstructing everything.

"The project can accumulate knowledge without every session accumulating all of
it." Judge a harness change against this rhythm first; the 2026-09-11
foundation question review ranks its options that way (conservation, then
continuity, then verified completion).

The ledger records it under `FND-Q04` as the answer to whether the Workbench is
only for software. The foundation rework's other owner answers carry stable IDs
(`FND-*`, `WF-*`, `TT-*`, `BPR-*`) in the
[grilling destination audit ledger](grilling-destination-audit-ledger.json):
