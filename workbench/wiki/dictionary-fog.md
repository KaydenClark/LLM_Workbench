# Fog: work you can see coming but cannot yet ask about

Fog is work that is expected on the way to a destination but cannot yet be
written as a precise decision question. The traveller knows something is
there; they cannot yet say what to decide about it. The canonical definition is
the [glossary entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#destination-and-direction).

**What it means here.** It becomes plannable when the question can be phrased, not only when its answer is known. Deliberately excluded work stays out of scope.

**Neighbouring words.** Fog is one of the three things a [Map](dictionary-map.md)
shows, beside [Decisions so far](dictionary-decisions-so-far.md) and
out-of-scope work. It is not an [Idea](dictionary-idea.md): an Idea is the
owner's starting thought, recorded as given, and Fog is the unphrased work
between an Idea and [Align](dictionary-align.md). When Fog can be phrased it
becomes a question for a [Destination Question Card](dictionary-destination-question-card.md)
or a Task at the [Frontier](dictionary-frontier.md).

**In use.** *Inference:* before the census of the Lexicon Retirement And ARCHITECTURE.md Spec (S-004O) ran, how much Wiki writing
the Lexicon's retirement needed was Fog: it was known to be there, but no one
could yet say how to slice it. Once the census counted about 118 glossary
explanations and 37 Wiki-only entries, the question could be phrased, and the
Wiki work was re-cut into five batches with the Wiki shape decision recorded
beside it.
