# Builder: delivering a scoped, verified result

Builder is the stance that delivers one scoped result, verified, with its documentation kept current. It is the usual stance of a Worker on a Task. The canonical definition is the [glossary entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

**What it means here.** Implementation includes relevant review and verification.

**Neighbouring words.** It is one of the four portable [Stance](dictionary-stance.md) skills, beside [Auditor](dictionary-auditor.md), [Reviewer](dictionary-reviewer.md) and [Reconciler](dictionary-reconciler.md). Its work is the [Journey](dictionary-journey.md): [Implement](dictionary-implement.md), [Check](dictionary-check.md), [QA](dictionary-qa.md) and [Submit](dictionary-submit.md). The skill reference page is [Builder](skill-builder.md).

**In use.** This Task runs in the Builder stance: it committed a failing test-wiki case for the batch-three terms first, then wrote the articles, inventory fields, routes and re-pointed checks until that case and the full suite passed, and submitted the branch with its two merge answers.
