# Decisions so far: the Map's index of conclusions

Decisions so far is the part of a Map that lists what has already been decided
on the way to its destination, each line short and linked to the artifact that
owns the decision and its evidence. The canonical definition is the
[glossary entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#destination-and-direction).

**What it means here.** Durable reasoning remains in its owning artifact; the Map does not copy it. The index exists so a later agent can
see at a glance what is settled without reopening it, and can follow the link
when it needs the reasoning.

**Neighbouring words.** It is one of the three things a [Map](dictionary-map.md)
shows around its destination, beside [Fog](dictionary-fog.md) (what cannot yet
be phrased as a question) and out-of-scope work. Settled decisions are
excluded from Fog. Because it only links, it is an instance of
[Traverse, don't search](dictionary-traverse-don-t-search.md).

**In use.** A Spec's Decisions And Contracts section plays this role: in the
Lexicon Retirement And ARCHITECTURE.md Spec (S-004O), "The Lexicon retires"
is one line linking its decision record, and the Wiki shape line names who
decided it and when. The `wayfinder` skill keeps a section with this name on the
maps it writes.
