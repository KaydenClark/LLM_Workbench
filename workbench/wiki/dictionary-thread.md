# Thread: related Chats taken together

A Thread is a connected series or tree of related Chats. The canonical
definition is the [glossary entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#chats-and-roles).

**What it means here.** It groups conversational continuity only. It does not replace a Spec, Task dependency, notepad, handoff, or project work owner.

**Neighbouring words.** It groups [Chats](dictionary-chat.md) and their
[Conversations](dictionary-conversation.md). The work they share is still owned
by a [Spec](dictionary-spec.md) and its [Tasks](dictionary-task.md).

**In use.** The Dispatcher's Chat for S-004O and the Worker Chats it started for
TK-009I and its siblings form one Thread. What each Worker needed still
travelled in its Task's Destination Packet, and what it did is in its Task
receipt and pull request, not in the Thread.
