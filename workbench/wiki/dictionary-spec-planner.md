# Spec Planner: planning one Spec at flight launch

Spec Planner is the stance that plans one Spec into small Tasks and parallel vertical slices when its flight launches. The canonical definition is the [glossary entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#stance-terms).

**What it means here.** A Dispatcher may dispatch Workers to help author Tasks, then reconciles their drafts and hands the plan to Spec Manager. Its operating capability is specified separately from the Dispatcher role.

**Neighbouring words.** It is a [Stance](dictionary-stance.md) the Dispatcher role uses; the plan it produces is handed to [Spec Manager](dictionary-spec-manager.md). Its output is the Spec's Tasks, cut at [Plan](dictionary-plan.md). The skill reference page is [Spec Planner](skill-spec-planner.md).

**In use.** When this Spec's census showed about 118 glossary explanations and 37 Wiki-only entries, the Dispatcher, in the Spec Planner stance, re-cut the single Wiki Task into five batches, one Task each, so that two could run at a time without writing the same lines, and recorded the change in the Spec's slice order.
