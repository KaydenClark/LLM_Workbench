# Idea: the owner's starting thought

Idea is the first workflow verb: the owner's starting thought, recorded as the
owner gave it, before anyone has aligned on it. The canonical definition is the
[glossary entry](../../GLOSSARY.md#workflow-verbs).

**What it means here.** Not Fog: Fog is anticipated work toward a destination that cannot yet be stated as a precise decision question. The owner's note: "Between idea and Align is the Fog of war mentioned in the Wayfind Skill." An Idea is Intent: it says what the
owner wants and authorizes nothing by itself.

**Neighbouring words.** It precedes [Align](dictionary-align.md), where it
becomes a [Design concept](dictionary-design-concept.md) through grilling, and
[Confirm](dictionary-confirm.md), where the owner agrees to the readback. The
unphrased work between the two is [Fog](dictionary-fog.md). It is the first
verb of the [Workflow](dictionary-workflow.md).

**In use.** The owner's 2026-10-05 message "we dont need runbook or lexicon. We
should be using the wiki for those things." is the Idea behind the Lexicon's
retirement: recorded as given, then aligned and confirmed before any Spec was
mapped.
