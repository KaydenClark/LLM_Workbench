# Workbench continuity through maintained owners

Continuity is the whole Workbench's product promise. A capable replacement
agent should recover the authorized objective, decisions, relevant knowledge,
achieved state and next action from maintained controls, specs, Wiki and source
with evidence. Objective notes preserve unfinished reasoning not yet reconciled
into those owners; they are one mechanism, not the owner of the whole promise.
Save during meaningful work and name the last confirmed saved boundary.

Considered alternatives: A transcript archive substitutes volume for maintained truth. Making the JSON
notepad alone own continuity drops code, controls and durable knowledge.
Mandatory coordination machinery would break standalone operation.

Consequences: A useful fresh-context task is required for a continuity claim. Capture quality,
actual recovery, interruption limits and cross-device transport are separate
proofs; no final write after Stop or recovery of unsaved state is promised.

Provenance: owner-requested v3.2.0 implementation and make-it-so promotion,
2026-09-08; reconciled concern CAND-A in
[S-050](../../specs/S-050-workbench-v3-2-0-release/SPEC.md).
Historical source statuses and lineage remain in its complete reconciliation.
Acceptance of this decision is not a claim its runtime or outcome is implemented.
