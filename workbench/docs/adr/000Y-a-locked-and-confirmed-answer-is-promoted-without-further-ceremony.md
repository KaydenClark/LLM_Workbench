# A locked and confirmed answer is promoted without further ceremony

## Decision

The owner confirmed these points in a grilling on 2026-10-02:

1. **Promote ends a grilling.** Promote is the end of a grilling.
2. **Decision records go through `to-docs`.** A DDR is a decision record, the
   same as an ADR, so `to-docs` composes it. `/to-dqc` still needs to be a
   skill.
3. **No extra ceremony.** Once an answer is locked and confirmed, it can be
   promoted. The owner: "We dont need extra ceremony", such as asking whether
   the notepad was marked as needing to update the DQC, which needed to update
   the notepad. He did not want what it means to grill and confirm a concept
   reopened.

Considered and rejected: extra bookkeeping between the notepad and the DQC
before promoting (the owner's example in point 3).

## Consequences

The Lexicon's Direct promotion definition and Decision Record row state this.
The owner floated renaming `to-docs` to `/to-DRs` or `/to-Records`, "if
anything"; no rename is decided. The `promote`, `to-docs` and `grilling` skills
are unchanged by this record.

An agent reading, not the owner's words: the grilling-destination ledger's GX-3
answer names `/to-dqc` the grilling-completion skill, and this record also makes
promote the end of a grilling; how the two compose is not decided here.
