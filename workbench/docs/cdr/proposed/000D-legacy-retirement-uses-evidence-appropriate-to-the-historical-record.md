# Legacy retirement uses evidence appropriate to the historical record

## Agent proposal

Retire a completed legacy Spec after verifying that its useful knowledge, corrections, unresolved obligations and traceability survive in the maintained owners, using evidence appropriate to that historical record. Do not claim a missing historical owner approval or rerun result.

## Underlying question and why

What proof is sufficient to retire historical scaffolding without either fabricating old QA or making the owner repeat every historical approval? GB-0020 and GB-0159 share the same unresolved policy choice. Their owning S-00Q record still controls execution and its concrete digest.

## Alternatives

Audit the capture, links and preserved gaps; reconstruct historical per-Spec QA when feasible; or retain the old records on the active roster. The draft recommends the capture audit, while recording absent historical proof explicitly. This recommendation is not owner-approved.

## Consequences and revisiting

If selected, apply the policy through S-00Q and the existing retirement operations. Preserve exceptions and unperformed proof. No Spec is retired by this draft and no retirement digest is approved. Revisit if capture loses an obligation or a required approval still belongs to the live work.
