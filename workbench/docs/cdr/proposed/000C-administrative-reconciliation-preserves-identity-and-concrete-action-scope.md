# Administrative reconciliation preserves identity and concrete action scope

## Agent proposal

Administrative reconciliation preserves stable identities, prior receipts and source lineage. A rename or obsolete-branch cleanup has a concrete before/after target and recoverable evidence. Routine reversible repairs within an existing assignment are derived from the existing controls; destructive cleanup still needs authorization for the specific action.

## Underlying question and why

What should agents protect when resolving administrative exceptions so the owner does not have to arbitrate every rename or stale branch? GB-0008 asks about a corrective Task rename; GB-0009 asks about one stale remote branch. Neither becomes a blanket cleanup instruction by being grouped here.

## Alternatives

Escalate every administrative discrepancy; derive reversible repairs from existing authority and escalate the actual destructive action; or preserve all abandoned branches indefinitely. The proposed middle policy preserves provenance and bounded autonomy.

## Consequences and revisiting

The concrete Task identity and branch evidence remain in their owning records. No branch is deleted and no rename is approved by this draft. Revisit if a proposed repair changes assignment, ownership or recoverability.
