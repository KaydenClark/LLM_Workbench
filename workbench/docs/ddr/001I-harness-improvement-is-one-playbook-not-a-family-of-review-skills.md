# Harness improvement is one playbook, not a family of review skills

Landmark: Learn from Failure.

Improving the harness around one observed job is one playbook skill with one loop: baseline, earliest gap, smallest owning intervention, native verification, fresh rerun, then retain, revise or remove. The harness-review family, fifteen skills installed in the owner's host skills root and none in the tracked lane, retires in its favor. This is the first move of retiring harness faster than adding it.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner, in his own words (2026-10-05): "I feel like the Playbook is what I was trying to have the RUNBOOK be, and we lost the design concept along the way".
- Readback item 6, the harness-review skills become one improve-one-harnessed-job skill, confirmed 2026-10-05.

Considered and rejected: keeping the harness-review family and adding the playbook beside it.

Consequences: a Spec ships the one skill in the tracked lane and records the host family as retired in its favor; the Runbook rows for the harness feedback loop and evaluation point at the one skill; the harness feedback review's read-only posture and its append-only feedback records keep their homes.

Provenance: the owner's review of Codex's recommendation and its readback, 2026-10-05, confirmed the same day. The loop's shape is Ryan Lopopolo's improve-harness playbook, linked and attributed in the Wiki lineage page.
