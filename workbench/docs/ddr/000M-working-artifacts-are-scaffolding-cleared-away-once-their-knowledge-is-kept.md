# Working artifacts are scaffolding, cleared away once their knowledge is kept

Landmark: Workbench artifacts.

Scaffolding is cleared away at Clean Up once its knowledge, unfinished obligations and needed evidence have reached their durable owners; a Spec or Task only after its change is verified on main. Nothing is archived. A later gap becomes a new Spec under the landmark or the Blueprint, never a revived record.

On the later-gap half, the owner confirmed on the round-6 card (2026-10-03): A later gap against delivered work becomes a new Spec under its landmark or the Blueprint; in the rare case, a landmark is reopened or a new one made. It is never a revived Spec and never a correction anchored to a Wiki claim. The Wiki holds knowledge, facts, opinions, summaries, entity and concept pages, comparisons, overviews and synthesis, and evidence for the direction and the plan, but it is not the source of the destination.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner, in his own words (round-1 review note, 2026-10-02): "I called them scaffolding specifically so I stopped calling them durable. If we intend to let workbench discard them, then they are not durable."
- The owner (ledger answer E-1): "SPECs and TASKs are transient... so we know they are not just done, but cleaned up."
- The owner (2026-09-16, ledger answer WF-8F): once turned into documentation a person can read, the source "is junk and clutter and may be thrown away."

Considered and rejected: None recorded.

Consequences: The owner stated that promotion changes AGENTS, the Lexicon Task and Destination Packet rows, and the Wiki-claim corrective records in the tooling. No other consequences are recorded.
