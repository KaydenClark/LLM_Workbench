# Every session works inside its smart zone and spends its tokens efficiently

Landmark: Context efficiency.

Work is divided and sized so every session does its part inside the smart zone. That is why work divides into landmarks, Specs and Tasks, why it is mapped and planned before it is implemented, and why the Journey runs as it does. Work larger than one smart zone is split at a natural boundary and handed to a fresh session. Every session spends its tokens efficiently, on the work rather than on rediscovery or ceremony.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner, in his own words (confirmed in chat, 2026-10-03): "The smart zone is why we create the Landmarks, Specs, Tasks. Its why we map and plan before we implement. Its why the journey is structured the way we have it."
- The owner's confirmed reason for one Task per chat: A chat does one Task, cleans up and ends, so every Task starts inside a fresh smart zone instead of inheriting a context the last Task already filled.
- On efficient spending, the owner, in his own words (2026-10-03): "We do not want to over spend on budget, and what we are spending on tokens on, should be efficient". Only this efficiency clause is used as a reason here, because the locked correction dropped the promise of proof of progress.
- On the size of the smart zone, the owner (2026-09-12): the size is "a good goalpost, not a gate or blocker"; "I was going to say 150k, but you have all gotten a lot better in the last month. So this grows over time."

Considered and rejected: The promise that every run leaves proof of the progress it made. The owner's locked correction (question BT-D20, "Yes, approved.", 2026-10-03) drops it "because a hard rule like that beats the work in the opposite direction".

Consequences: None recorded as owner words.
