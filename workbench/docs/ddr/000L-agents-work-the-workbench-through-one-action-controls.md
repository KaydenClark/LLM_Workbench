# Agents work the workbench through one-action controls

Landmark: Enduring context.

Agents work the workbench through controls, each one action, and open the underlying record only to verify a claim, audit, debug a control, or when a control is missing or disagrees with its record.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner, in his own words (teardown round 1, 2026-10-02), describing the notepad: "like a voice note. they push a button on the workbench, speak, it records what they said, and they go back to work."
- The owner corrected the word "button" (teardown round 2, 2026-10-03): "I dont want an actual button, but I want something that functions with that same ease of use. A tool we can (Action) and move on." The owner named "Controls" as the better word, which is why this record says controls, each one action.

Considered and rejected: Calling them buttons. The owner: "calling them actual buttons is what I dont like."

Consequences: None recorded.

Provenance: the Blueprint teardown grilling of 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-03: question BT-D0 (one-action controls), locked 2026-10-03 on the round-6 card; reasons from the owner's own words, question WHY-B9. The decision was first worded in the owner's DDR and control-surface grilling of 2026-10-01 (question CTL-1).
