# Prototype needs no map and lands nothing in enduring context

Landmark: Workflow.

Prototype is an optional workflow verb. It needs no map, and what it produces does not land in enduring context, because nobody has approved how it works yet. It can come before Confirm or between Confirm and Plan: Align, Prototype, Align, Confirm; or Align, Prototype, Confirm; or Confirm, Prototype, Map; or Confirm, Map, Prototype, Map, Plan. Prototype code carries forward into the product only once it meets the same implementation and verification requirements as any other work; on a fresh project it can live in a separate codebase or a proving stack.

Why the owner chose it: The owner's confirmed reason: Prototype code has not passed the checks that make other work trustworthy, so it enters the product only through them.

Considered and rejected: None recorded.

Consequences: None recorded as owner words. Two accepted records differ from this one, stated as fact and not resolved here: [Blueprint, Spec and Task are three altitudes of one delivery chain](../../adr/000G-blueprint-spec-and-task-are-three-altitudes-of-one-delivery-chain.md) places an optional prototype after the Blueprint and before a Spec, and [The workflow is eight verbs and each verb writes the plane its claims live on](../../adr/000X-the-workflow-is-eight-verbs-and-each-verb-writes-the-plane-its-claims-live-on.md) lists eight workflow verbs without Prototype. The owner's placement and verb here are the later lock; the workflow-verb and terms work carries the verb list.
