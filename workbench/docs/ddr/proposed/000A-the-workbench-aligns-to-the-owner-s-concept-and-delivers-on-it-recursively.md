# The Workbench aligns to the owner's concept and delivers on it recursively

Landmark: Workflow.

The Workbench aligns with the owner's concept and then delivers on that aligned concept recursively: the Journey runs, the owner approves the result or sends it back, and the loop runs again until the concept is realized. Carrying one idea to one verified delivery is one turn of that loop, not the goal.

Why the owner chose it: The owner's confirmed reason: One turn of the loop may not produce something viable, and sending it back is how the system fixes the work without the owner specifying it a second time.

Considered and rejected: None recorded.

Consequences: None recorded as owner words.
