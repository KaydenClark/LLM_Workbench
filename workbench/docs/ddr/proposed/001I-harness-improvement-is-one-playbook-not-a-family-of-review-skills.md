# Harness improvement is one playbook, not a family of review skills

Landmark: Learn from Failure.

Improving the harness around one observed job is one playbook skill with one loop: baseline, earliest gap, smallest owning intervention, native verification, fresh rerun, then retain, revise or remove. The fourteen harness-review skills retire into it. This is the first move of retiring harness faster than adding it.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner, in his own words (2026-10-05): "I feel like the Playbook is what I was trying to have the RUNBOOK be, and we lost the design concept along the way".
- Readback item 6, fourteen harness-review skills become one improve-one-harnessed-job skill, confirmed 2026-10-05.

Considered and rejected: keeping the harness-review family and adding the playbook beside it.

Consequences: a Spec consolidates the skills and retires the rest; the Runbook row for harness evaluation points at the one skill; the harness feedback review's read-only posture and its append-only feedback records keep their homes.

Provenance: the owner's review of Codex's recommendation and its readback, 2026-10-05, confirmed the same day. The loop's shape is Ryan Lopopolo's improve-harness playbook, linked and attributed in the Wiki lineage page.
