# The Blueprint keeps its name and decision records are the design-doc layer

Landmark: Repo is the System of Record.

`BLUEPRINT.md` keeps its name and its job: the product spec, the destination. It is not a design document. The OpenAI knowledge base's kinds map onto what the Workbench already has: product spec to the Blueprint, design docs one per decision to DDRs and ADRs, exec plans to landmarks, Specs and Tasks, references to the Wiki, quality score to doctor and the guardrail baseline.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner asked (2026-10-05): "And our BLUEPRINT.md is basically DESIGN.md right?" and, shown the mapping, answered: "Yes, Keep its name. Yes, DDRs, ADRs, that sounds great. If not, decision records is what I have been calling them."

Considered and rejected: renaming the Blueprint to DESIGN.md or adding a DESIGN.md beside it.

Consequences: no file is renamed; the four-part Blueprint decision ([Every room's Blueprint is the four-part short page](000N-every-room-s-blueprint-is-the-four-part-short-page.md)) stands; the Wiki lineage page records the mapping so an agent arriving from the article finds the Workbench's equivalents.

Provenance: the owner's answer on the matklad post and the OpenAI layout, 2026-10-05, confirmed the same day.
