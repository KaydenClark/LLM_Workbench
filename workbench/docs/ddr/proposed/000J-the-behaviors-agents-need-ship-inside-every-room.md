# The behaviors agents need ship inside every room

Landmark: Work from anywhere.

The reusable behaviors agents need ship inside every room and are found from a fresh clone, by any supported harness; a personal catalog is a backup, never a dependency.

The owner's note: "The goal is behaviors ship, and are regulated and monitored by the workbench. There wouldn't be a non goal. This is part of success."

Why the owner chose it: The owner, in his own words (2026-09-22, portable workbench): "When we update the workbenches, we update the skills. LLM_Workbench owns the skills it needs to. We store them all in workbench/skills." The owner would keep the catalog "as a backup".

Considered and rejected: None recorded.

Consequences: None recorded as owner words. The shipping, the discovery from a fresh clone and the catalog's role as a backup are already recorded by [Core skills ship in the workbench skills lane](../../adr/000M-core-skills-ship-in-the-workbench-skills-lane.md) and are not restated here.
