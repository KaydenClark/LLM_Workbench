# Only a confirmed concept is mapped, planned or implemented

Landmark: Workflow.

A concept is aligned first, then confirmed with a readback the owner agrees to, naming the concept, its direction, its cost, the reason for it, and what will be created as a result. Only a confirmed concept is mapped, planned or implemented; a finished document is not agreement.

Why the owner chose it: The owner's confirmed reason: Nothing should land as durable before the owner has approved how it works, which is the same reason Prototype lands nothing in enduring context.

Considered and rejected: None recorded.

Consequences: None recorded as owner words.
