# The twelve harness-engineering directions are landmarks

Landmark: Blueprint (the landmark set itself).

Twelve directions become landmarks: Repo is the System of Record, Progressive Disclosure, Durable Plans, Agent to Agent Review, Mechanical Enforcement, Learn from Failure, Agent Visible Runtime, Continuous Cleanup, Human Attention Minimized, Multi Agent and Provider Coordination, Owner Idea Alignment, and Autonomous Execution. They are not the only landmarks. They are authored first, and the existing landmark records settle into them. The repository as system of record and mechanical enforcement are the foundation already in hand; the rest are what the Workbench still has to make.

Why the owner chose it:

- The owner, in his own words (2026-10-05): "I counted 12. Repo is the system of record, Progressive Disclosure, Durable Plans, Agent to Agent Review, Mechanical Enforcement, Learn from failure, Agent visable runtime, Continuous cleanup, Human Attention Miminimized, Multi Agent/provider Coordination, Owner Idea, Alignment. These are not the only landmarks, they should just be landmarks, they are good directions."
- On the twelfth (2026-10-05): "We can call the landmark Autonomous Execution. But, since its a destination we are heading towards for 'humans to steer, and agents to execute', and it was a destination we made along the way, lets add it. That way we also have a defined landmark to point back to when we need to fix the Task Execution."
- On order and overlap (2026-10-05): "I exepect the overlap. Let's do these first, that way we can settle the others into here. I feel like these are much better defined than what we would of made. It gives us real expectations."
- (2026-10-05): "The Repo is the System of record, mechanical enforement. The things in the distance the make the workbench what it is, that we need to make."

Considered and rejected: the eight evaluation questions as the landmark set, which the agent proposed as more testable; the owner kept the twelve comparison categories.

Consequences: the `LANDMARK.md` artifact is delivered first and the twelve are its first proof; the existing landmark records fold into the twelve where they overlap rather than standing beside them; the `LMK-` prefix stays and a landmark lists its decision records as a linked section, the agent's assumptions on the two open points of the landmark decision ([Landmarks are LANDMARK.md artifacts one size above Specs](../adr/000U-landmarks-are-landmark-md-artifacts-one-size-above-specs.md)), confirmed with the sequencing.
